Asset Class Risk & History Breakdown

1. No to Low Risk (FD, RD, Debt Funds, Gold)

Designed for capital preservation and emergency reserves. Guaranteed safety or low volatility, backed by secure banking structures or stable sovereign assets.

10-Yr Historic Avg6.0% - 7.5%
15-Yr Historic Avg6.5% - 7.2%
20-Yr Historic Avg7.0% - 7.5%

2. Low to Moderate Risk (Hybrid Funds, Multi-Asset Allocation)

Engineered for balanced growth by dynamically merging stable debt instruments with equities or commodities.

10-Yr Historic Avg10.0% - 11.5%
15-Yr Historic Avg10.5% - 12.0%
20-Yr Historic Avg11.0% - 12.5%

3. Moderate to High Risk (Flexi Cap, ETFs, Direct Equities)

Optimized for aggressive wealth creation over long-term compounding horizons. Substantial short-term volatility, but historically outperforms inflation significantly over 10+ years.

10-Yr Historic Avg13.0% - 15.5%
15-Yr Historic Avg13.5% - 16.0%
20-Yr Historic Avg14.0% - 16.5%
