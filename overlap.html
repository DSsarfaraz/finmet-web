How to Export Portfolio Statement File

Groww
  1. Open Groww App/Web and click your Profile avatar.
  2. Go to Reports → Stock / MF Holdings Statement.
  3. Select Download Excel / CSV.
Zerodha (Coin & Console)
  1. Log in to Console (console.zerodha.com).
  2. Click Portfolio → Holdings.
  3. Click Download / Export (CSV or Excel).
Angel One
  1. Go to Portfolio section on App/Web.
  2. Tap the Download Icon in top corner.
  3. Choose Holdings Summary (CSV/Excel).
ICICI Direct & CAMS / KFintech CAS
  1. ICICI Direct: Portfolio → Equity/MF → Export to Excel.
  2. CAMS / MF Central: Request Consolidated Account Statement (CAS) in Excel format.
